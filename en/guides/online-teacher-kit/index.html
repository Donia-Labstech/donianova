The online teacher's starter kit

What a teacher actually needs to give a remote lesson with clear sound, a comfortable posture and visual explanations, in order of priority and with options for a small budget.

Home/The online teacher's starter kit

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Start with sound, not the camera

Pupils tolerate an average picture, but not choppy or distant sound. Wireless earbuds with a microphone near your mouth improve a lesson more than any expensive webcam.

Choose earbuds with recent Bluetooth and a stable connection, and make sure they stay comfortable for a full hour.

Posture: a laptop stand

Hours in front of a laptop flat on the desk mean a bent neck. A stand that lifts the screen to eye level reduces fatigue and puts the camera in front of your face instead of under your chin.

If you raise the laptop, use a separate keyboard and mouse when you can.

More ports: a USB hub

Modern laptops have few ports. A hub lets you connect a mouse, a keyboard and a memory card reader at once.

Note: to connect your laptop to a classroom projector, choose a hub with an HDMI port.

Visual explanations: writing pad or tablet

  • LCD writing pad: cheap, no glowing screen, great for drafts and quick sums, but it cannot save or send what you write.
  • Tablet: lets you annotate slides and share your screen in class, but costs more and needs setting up.

On a tight budget, start with the pad and photograph what you write with your phone.

What to buy first

  • 1. Earbuds (most important).
  • 2. Laptop stand.
  • 3. USB hub if your ports are not enough.
  • 4. Writing pad or tablet for visual explanations.

Frequently asked questions

Are ordinary phone earphones enough?

They work, but a distant microphone picks up room noise. Wireless earbuds with a close microphone sound clearer.

Does the hub work with a projector?

Only if it has an HDMI port. The hub we list right now has no HDMI.

LCD pad or tablet?

The pad suits quick drafts and small budgets; the tablet suits teachers who want to annotate and share lessons live.
