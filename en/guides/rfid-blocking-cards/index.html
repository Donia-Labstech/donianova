RFID blocking cards: do you really need one?

An honest guide for anyone carrying contactless bank cards or a modern passport: how blocking works, when it helps, and how to choose and use a blocking card properly.

Home/Best RFID blocking cards and wallets

Last updated: 2026-11-08

What is RFID and NFC skimming?

Tap-to-pay cards and biometric passports carry a small chip that works at 13.56 MHz. When a reader comes close, the chip exchanges some data without contact.

A blocking card uses a metal layer that interferes with this frequency, so nearby readers struggle to talk to the cards next to it.

How big is the risk, really?

Honestly, wireless card theft on the street is rare: a reader has to get very close, and the data it can read is limited and usually excludes your PIN.

Still, a blocking card is cheap and light, and gives peace of mind in crowded places such as airports, public transport and markets. Think of it as a simple precaution, not an essential.

Blocking card or RFID wallet?

  • Blocking card: goes into the wallet you already own, cheap, protects only the cards right next to it.
  • RFID wallet: shielding is built into every slot, so all cards are covered, but it costs more and means replacing your wallet.

If your wallet is fine and you want a quick fix, start with a card.

How to use it properly

  • Put the card in the same slot as your bank cards, or between two of them.
  • Do not expect it to protect a card in another pocket.
  • Test it yourself: hold your closed wallet to a payment terminal. If the terminal cannot read your card, the blocking works.
  • Take your card out of the wallet when you pay by tap.

What we avoid when choosing

  • Claims such as "100% protection" or "the strongest technology in the world": no protection is absolute.
  • Very thick cards that stretch your wallet.
  • Listings without clear photos of the card itself or without real reviews.

Frequently asked questions

Does a blocking card harm my bank card?

No. It does not erase or damage your card; it only stops wireless reading while it sits next to it.

Does it protect a passport?

Yes, if you place it against the chip page inside the passport cover.

Do I need one per bank card?

Usually one card protects the cards right next to it in the same slot.
