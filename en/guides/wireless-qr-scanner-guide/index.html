How to choose a wireless QR scanner for your shop or office (2026 guide)

A practical guide for shops, pharmacies, bookstores and offices: the difference between wireless scanner types, the specs that actually matter, and how to avoid the most common setup problem.

Home/Best wireless QR scanners for shops

Last updated: 2026-11-14

First: 1D or 2D?

A 1D scanner (laser or CCD) reads classic line barcodes only. It cannot read QR codes at all.

A 2D imager reads both line barcodes and QR codes, including from a phone screen, which you need for tickets, coupons, e-payments and QR attendance cards.

Our advice: pick 2D even if you only use line barcodes today; the price gap is now small.

Second: 2.4G, Bluetooth, or both?

  • 2.4G: comes with a small USB receiver. Works instantly with no setup and usually has more range. Good for shops and storerooms.
  • Bluetooth: connects straight to a phone, tablet or computer with no receiver. Good if you work in a phone app.
  • Tri-mode (2.4G + Bluetooth + USB cable): the most flexible; if the battery dies, use it wired.

Note: range figures in listings (e.g. 100 m) are measured in open space. Indoors, expect much less, which is usually still plenty.

Third: the specs that actually matter

  • Screen reading: make sure the listing mentions reading codes from phone screens.
  • Storage / offline mode: keeps scans when you walk out of range and sends them later. Useful for stocktaking.
  • Battery: 1500–2500 mAh covers a full working day in most shops.
  • Durability: look for a drop rating (e.g. 1.5 m); scanners get dropped a lot.
  • Stand: some come with a cradle that turns on hands-free auto-scan, handy at the checkout.

The most common problem: AZERTY keyboards

The scanner types like a keyboard. If your computer uses a French AZERTY layout and the scanner is set to US QWERTY, you get symbols like "à&é" instead of digits.

The fix: scan the "French keyboard / AZERTY" setup code in the scanner manual once, or switch your input language to English while scanning.

No manual? Ask the seller for the PDF on AliExpress before you buy.

What we avoid when choosing

  • A 1D scanner sold as "reads every code".
  • Products rated under 85% or with almost no sales.
  • Promises like "reads any damaged code" or "unlimited range".

Frequently asked questions

Does a wireless scanner need drivers?

Usually not. Most work as a keyboard (HID) with Windows and phones. Click into the field and scan.

Will it work with my billing or stock software?

If your software accepts typed codes from a keyboard, the scanner will work. Rare exceptions need a special scanner interface.

How is it different from a ring scanner?

A ring scanner is worn on a finger and leaves both hands free, ideal for picking and packing, but it has a smaller battery and usually costs more.

How long does the battery last?

It depends on the model and scan count. Most last a working day and recharge overnight over USB.
