RFID & NFC

RFID & NFC blocking card

A card-sized shield you slip next to your contactless bank cards or passport. It targets the 13.56 MHz signal that tap-to-pay cards use, so the cards beside it are harder to read without your knowledge. Keep it in the same pocket or slot as the cards it protects.

Best for: Wallets, passports, travel View on AliExpress

Lenovo thinkplus TWS earbuds

Bluetooth 5.3 wireless earbuds with touch controls, deep bass and noise reduction, from a known brand.

Classroom tech

8-in-2 USB hub with card reader

One aluminium hub for laptops and tablets: plugs in by USB-C or USB-A, adds 4 USB ports, SD and microSD slots, a USB-C port and a 3.5 mm audio jack. It has no HDMI, so it cannot connect a projector.

Best for: Teachers' laptops, tablets View on AliExpress
Classroom tech

Foldable laptop stand, 10 heights

Raises the screen from 10.5 to 20 cm in 10 steps so you look ahead instead of down, and lets air flow under the laptop. Folds flat into a bag. It is ABS plastic, not aluminium: fine for everyday laptops, less suited to heavy gaming models.

Best for: Teachers, remote work, students View on AliExpress
Classroom tech

Colour LCD writing tablet, 13 or 16 inch

A paperless board for writing, counting and drawing that clears with one button. A lock switch stops accidental erasing, and the pen clips into the frame. It cannot save drawings, and the erase button wipes the whole screen at once.

Best for: Children 3–12, tutoring, drills View on AliExpress
Classroom tech

11.5-inch LCD writing pad with folio case

A thin, full-screen pad for notes, sums and lesson sketches, aimed at adults. The folio case folds into a stand and holds the pen; a lock switch prevents accidental erasing; a coin battery lasts about a year. It has no backlight, so it is hard to read in dim light, and it cannot save what you write.

Best for: Teachers, students, meeting notes View on AliExpress
Classroom tech

Lenovo Xiaoxin Pad 11 (2025), 6 GB + 128 GB

An 11-inch Android tablet with a sharp 2560×1600, 90 Hz screen, a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chip, four speakers and a 7040 mAh battery, plus a microSD slot. Good for lessons, reading and video. It is the Chinese version: it ships with Chinese firmware, usually without Google Play preinstalled, and it is Wi-Fi only with no SIM slot. Check that you are comfortable setting it up before buying.

Best for: Students, teachers, reading and video View on AliExpress
Smart home

Smart Bluetooth body scale

Syncs your weight to a phone app over Bluetooth, with 4 sensors, 50 g steps up to 180 kg, and USB charging instead of batteries. Body-fat figures are estimates; weight is the reliable number.

Best for: Families, fitness tracking View on AliExpress
Travel tech

Pocket monocular with phone clip

A 9 cm monocular that fits in a pocket, with a clip to hold it over your phone camera. Good for hikes, concerts and birdwatching. Ignore the "2000x" on the listing: expect the modest zoom of a small lens, not a telescope for stars.

Best for: Hiking, concerts, birdwatching View on AliExpress
Travel tech

V500 OBD2 car diagnostic scanner

A handheld scanner with its own colour screen: plug it into the OBD2 port under the dashboard to read and clear engine fault codes, see live data and check battery voltage. No phone or app needed, so it works the same for Android and iPhone users. It reads standard engine codes only, not ABS or airbag systems, and clearing a code does not fix the fault behind it. Menus come in 8 languages including English and French, but not Arabic.

Best for: Car owners, road trips, used-car checks View on AliExpress
