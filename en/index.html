RFID & NFC

RFID & NFC blocking card

A card-sized shield you slip next to your contactless bank cards or passport. It targets the 13.56 MHz signal that tap-to-pay cards use, so the cards beside it are harder to read without your knowledge. Keep it in the same pocket or slot as the cards it protects.

Foldable laptop stand, 10 heights

Classroom tech

11.5-inch LCD writing pad with folio case

A thin, full-screen pad for notes, sums and lesson sketches, aimed at adults. The folio case folds into a stand and holds the pen; a lock switch prevents accidental erasing; a coin battery lasts about a year. It has no backlight, so it is hard to read in dim light, and it cannot save what you write.

Pocket monocular with phone clip

A 9 cm monocular that fits in a pocket, with a clip to hold it over your phone camera. Good for hikes, concerts and birdwatching. Ignore the "2000x" on the listing: expect the modest zoom of a small lens, not a telescope for stars.

Best for: Hiking, concerts, birdwatching View on AliExpress
Travel tech

V500 OBD2 car diagnostic scanner

A handheld scanner with its own colour screen: plug it into the OBD2 port under the dashboard to read and clear engine fault codes, see live data and check battery voltage. No phone or app needed, so it works the same for Android and iPhone users. It reads standard engine codes only, not ABS or airbag systems, and clearing a code does not fix the fault behind it. Menus come in 8 languages including English and French, but not Arabic.

Best for: Car owners, road trips, used-car checks View on AliExpress
