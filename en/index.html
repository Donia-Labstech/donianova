RFID & NFC

RFID & NFC blocking card

A card-sized shield you slip next to your contactless bank cards or passport. It targets the 13.56 MHz signal that tap-to-pay cards use, so the cards beside it are harder to read without your knowledge. Keep it in the same pocket or slot as the cards it protects.
